Is Guidera free?+

Yes — free to download with generous free use. A subscription unlocks unlimited tours and longer-form narratives.

Tours and saved places work offline once downloaded. New AI narrations require a connection.

Which languages are supported?+

Over 30 languages, with native-sounding voices in each.

Location is processed on-device for nearby suggestions. We never sell or share your data.
