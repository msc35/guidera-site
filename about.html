About

A travel app that whispers, not shouts.

I built Guidera for the kind of traveler who'd rather get pleasantly lost than follow a flag on a stick.

The problem with most travel apps

To me, a lot of travel apps feel like checklists. They send you to the same ten viewpoints, and when you do want to know what you're looking at, you end up reading a long article on your phone instead of looking up.

A different approach

Guidera assumes you're curious and capable. Tap any place on the map, anywhere in the world. Hear its story. Walk on.

Because an AI guide is only useful if you can trust it, every story is checked first. Guidera researches the place from Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap and official city websites, and a verifier removes anything those sources don't support. When a place is little known, the guide says less instead of making things up.

Built by one person

Guidera is made by Mehmet Selim Çetin, a solo developer in Munich who got tired of squinting at Wikipedia in the rain. I design, build and maintain the app myself, from the curated tours to the way each story is checked.

If you have feedback, an idea, or just want to say hi, write to mehmetselim692@icloud.com. Real human, real reply.
