FAQ

Good questions, honestly answered.

Getting started

What is Guidera?+

Guidera is an iPhone AI tour guide that tells source-checked stories about any place on the map, in 9 languages.

Is Guidera free?+

Yes, it’s free to download. The free tier includes 5 AI stories a day (up to 20 by watching short ads), text stories, Arthur the Historian, the Informative and Playful tones, and banner ads.

What does Premium include, and what does it cost?+

Unlimited stories, audio narration, Walkman mode, custom tours, follow-up questions, all guides and tones, and no ads. Premium is sold as passes, not a subscription: €1 for 3 days, €3 for a week or €7 for a month. Passes stack.

Which devices does it support?+

iPhone with iOS 18.5 or later, plus an Apple Watch companion. There is no Android version.

Do I need an account?+

No. Guidera has no user accounts, so there’s nothing to sign up for.

Stories & tours

How do I know the stories are accurate?+

Before narrating, Guidera researches the place from Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap and official city websites. A verifier removes anything the sources don’t support. For little-known places, the guide says less rather than making things up.

Does it only work in big cities?+

No. Stories work on any place Apple Maps knows, worldwide, not just famous landmarks. Curated walking tours are available in 35 cities; see Cities.

What are custom tours?+

With Premium, Guidera can plan a walking tour for you, built only from real places. Like curated tours, you can stop and resume where you left off.

What is Walkman mode?+

A Premium feature for tours: turn it on, pocket your phone, and Guidera narrates each stop automatically as you walk.

Can I choose a guide style?+

Yes. Pick one of three guides (Arthur the Historian, Max the Local Insider, Luna the Artist), one of four tones (Informative, Playful, Dark Humor, Calm) and one of three lengths (Short, Medium, Long). Arthur, Informative and Playful are free; the rest come with Premium.

Can I ask follow-up questions?+

Yes, with Premium. Type a question about the place and the guide answers.

On the road

Does it work offline?+

Not yet. New stories need an internet connection.

Which languages are supported?+

9 languages, for both the app and the stories: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish and Simplified Chinese.

Does it work on Apple Watch and the Lock Screen?+

Yes. There’s an Apple Watch companion, and with Premium a Live Activity shows your tour on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Privacy & data

Does Guidera use my location?+

To create a story, Guidera sends only the coordinates of the place you tapped, never your own location.

What data do you store about me?+

There are no user accounts. The free tier shows ads. For full details on how data is handled, see the Privacy Policy.
