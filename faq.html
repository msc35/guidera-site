FAQ

Good questions, honestly answered.

Getting started

Is Guidera free?+

Yes — free to download with generous free use. A subscription unlocks unlimited tours and longer-form narratives.

Which devices does it support?+

iPhone (iOS 18+), with full Apple Watch and Live Activity support. iPad works too. An Android version is on the roadmap.

Do I need an account?+

No. You can use Guidera anonymously. An optional account lets you sync favorites and history across devices.

On the road

Does it work offline?+

Saved tours and previously visited places work offline. Generating new narratives requires a connection.

Which languages are supported?+

9 languages, with native-sounding voices in each. You can switch languages anytime.

Can I choose a guide style?+

Yes — from concise to deeply academic, from local-friend casual to art-historian formal. Pick a persona, dial the depth.

Will it drain my battery?+

It's designed to be light. Pocket your phone, lock the screen, and Guidera continues narrating with minimal drain.

Privacy & data

Do you sell my location data?+

No. Never. Location is processed to surface nearby places and improve narration relevance — and that's it.

Where is my data stored?+

Account data is encrypted at rest. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I delete my data?+

Yes, anytime, from inside the app. We honor full account deletion.
