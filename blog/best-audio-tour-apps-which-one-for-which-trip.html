Audio tour apps compared: which one fits your trip?

By Mehmet Selim Çetin, developer of Guidera · Published 1 October 2026 · Last updated 1 October 2026 · 8 min read

There is no single best audio tour app; it depends on the trip. For offline, story-led walking tours made by local storytellers, VoiceMap fits best. For a free audio companion in Europe, Rick Steves Audio Europe. For self-guided walking routes in many cities, GPSmyCity. For road trips and national parks, GuideAlong. For tapping any building or square and hearing a source-checked story about it, Guidera, the app I build.

Disclosure: I'm the developer of Guidera, one of the apps below. Every fact about the other apps comes from their App Store listing and official website, checked on 1 October 2026. There are no affiliate links, and I haven't compared ratings or reviews. Details and prices change, so check each app before you buy.

Is VoiceMap good for offline walking tours?

VoiceMap offers GPS-triggered audio tours produced by local storytellers, including journalists, novelists, podcasters and tour guides. Its website lists 2,318 tours in 86 countries. After you download a tour, VoiceMap works offline and includes an offline map, and you can stop and resume where you left off. The app is free; tours are free or paid, and VoiceMap also sells tour passes. It is available on iOS and Android.

Limitation: VoiceMap covers a place only if someone has produced a tour there.

Sources: App Store listing, official site (checked 1 October 2026)

Is Rick Steves Audio Europe really free?

Yes. Rick Steves Audio Europe is a free app for iOS and Android that organises Rick Steves' audio into playlists by destination. It includes self-guided walking tours taken from his guidebooks and interviews from his public radio programme Travel with Rick Steves. Audio is downloaded to your phone, so no Wi-Fi or mobile connection is needed to listen.

Limitation: it is designed for Europe, and the App Store lists English as its only language.

Sources: App Store listing, official site (checked 1 October 2026)

Is GPSmyCity an audio tour app?

GPSmyCity offers self-guided walking tours and travel articles for more than 1,800 cities, with over 7,000 walks according to its website. The app is designed to work offline with offline city maps. It is free to download; the full versions of the walks and articles need an annual subscription, which the US App Store lists at $14.99 for walks or articles and $20.99 for everything, with a 3-day free trial. It is available on iOS and Android.

Limitation: the App Store listing describes voice-guided turn-by-turn directions as part of the paid upgrade, rather than narrated stories, and route maps also need the upgrade.

Sources: App Store listing, official site (checked 1 October 2026)

What is SmartGuide best for?

SmartGuide describes itself as a personal audio tour guide with travel guides for more than 1,000 popular destinations on its App Store listing, and says on its website that it covers over 100,000 places with AI-assisted, multilingual audio. Its narration plays automatically when you reach a sight. The app is free, and offline maps and guides come with its Premium option. It is available on iOS and Android, and the App Store lists 13 app languages.

Limitation: offline use needs Premium, and the Premium price isn't shown on SmartGuide's website.

Sources: App Store listing, official site (checked 1 October 2026)

Which audio tour app is best for road trips?

GuideAlong is built mainly for driving tours through national parks and along scenic routes, and also offers some walking tours. Its website lists 98 destinations across the US, Canada, Mexico, Iceland, Scotland and Australia. Commentary plays automatically based on GPS, and tours work offline once downloaded. The app is free; tours and bundles are one-time purchases, from free to $24.99, with no subscription. It is available on iOS and Android.

Limitation: GuideAlong is mostly about driving routes, and most of its destinations are in North America.

Sources: App Store listing, official site (checked 1 October 2026)

Which app can tell me about any building I'm looking at?

That is what Guidera is for. Guidera is an iPhone AI tour guide that tells source-checked stories about any place on the map, in 9 languages. You tap any place Apple Maps knows, not only stops on a fixed tour. Before narrating, Guidera researches the place from Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap and official city websites, and a verifier removes anything those sources don't support. You can pick one of three guides (Arthur the Historian, Max the Local Insider or Luna the Artist) and one of four tones. There are also 39 curated walking tours in 35 cities.

The free tier includes 5 AI stories a day (up to 20 with short ads). Premium adds audio narration, Walkman mode for tours, custom tours and follow-up questions, and is sold as passes rather than a subscription: €1 for 3 days, €3 for a week or €7 for a month.

Limitations: Guidera doesn't work offline, since new stories need a connection. It runs on iPhone (iOS 18.5 or later) with an Apple Watch companion, but not on Android.

Sources: App Store listing, Guidera features and pricing, how Guidera checks its stories

Frequently asked questions

Which audio tour apps work offline?+

VoiceMap, Rick Steves Audio Europe, GPSmyCity and GuideAlong work offline once you have downloaded the content, and SmartGuide offers offline guides with Premium. Guidera does not work offline.

Is there a free audio tour app?+

Rick Steves Audio Europe is free. VoiceMap and GuideAlong offer some free tours alongside paid ones, and Guidera has a free tier with 5 AI stories a day.

Which audio tour apps work on Android?+

VoiceMap, Rick Steves Audio Europe, GPSmyCity, SmartGuide and GuideAlong are available on Android. Guidera is iPhone only.

Can an app tell me about any building, not just famous landmarks?+

Guidera is designed for that: you can tap any place Apple Maps knows and get a story checked against sources. Tour-based apps such as VoiceMap and GuideAlong cover the places on their tours.

Which audio tour app is best for road trips?+

GuideAlong focuses on driving tours through national parks and scenic routes, with GPS-triggered commentary that works offline.
