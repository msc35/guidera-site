Features

Designed to disappear.

Every detail is here so you can stop staring at your screen and start looking up.

On the map

Tap anywhere. Hear its story.

Any place Apple Maps knows is a story waiting: a cathedral, a park, a statue, a side street, anywhere in the world. Tap it and Guidera researches the place from Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap and official city websites, then tells its story. A verifier removes anything the sources don't support. No more skimming Wikipedia in the rain.

On the move

Pocket-it audio that just plays. Premium

Press play and listen to any story. On a tour, Walkman mode narrates each stop automatically as you walk, like a podcast that follows your feet. Stop anytime and resume where you left off. A Live Activity keeps the tour on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and the Apple Watch companion keeps it on your wrist.

On your terms

Ask anything. Get a real answer. Premium

Type a follow-up question about the place. Curious about the architect? The year it was built? What the statue out front means? Guidera answers in conversation, not bullet points.

Your guide, your way

Pick who tells the story, and how.

Three guides

Arthur the Historian, Max the Local Insider and Luna the Artist. Arthur is free; Max and Luna come with Premium.

Four tones, three lengths

Informative, Playful, Dark Humor or Calm, in Short, Medium or Long. Informative and Playful are free; Dark Humor and Calm come with Premium.

9 languages

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish and Simplified Chinese, for both the app and the stories.
